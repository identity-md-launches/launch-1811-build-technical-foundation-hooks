Binding an answer to its request
#
The callback's 
requestId
 is the intake's and the attestation's is the oracle's; the writer pairs the two. A contract that asks a different question per request, one per user, review or claim, takes that pairing out of what it trusts: it writes the request's identifiers into the question, stores the question's hash when it asks, and in the callback requires
 
a.questionHash
 to match. The hash is signed with the answer, so an answer to another request's question is refused whoever delivers it, and so is one to a question someone else paid for.
questionHash
 is the keccak-256 of the question document: compact JSON, keys sorted, strings as
 
JSON.stringify
 writes them, integers in decimal. Its members are 
answerType
,
 
chainId
, 
definitions
 when there are any, 
evidence
 only when it is
 
"panel"
, 
head

question
,
