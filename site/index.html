IMD job status

Read directly from the HookJobRequester contract over JSON-RPC (eth_call and eth_getLogs). No backend, and no request to api.imd.fun. "Review passed" means a signed panel answer matched the pending review's owner-pinned question and oracle identity. An answer awaiting binding or correction stays pending. It is not an audit. The contract owner controls binding and can replace the trusted Intake or signer, including for pending requests. The owner can explicitly revoke retained answers from a compromised signer epoch; finalized verdicts remain historical.

Delivery is not review approval. The result hash commits to the result document's bytes. The result URI appears in event history as unverified text; this page does not open it, fetch it or verify its contents. Include the delivery block in the history range to see it.
